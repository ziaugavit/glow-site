1. Safety and health

Glow's light sessions use flickering light from your iPhone's flashlight. Flickering light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, sometimes without any previous history.

  • Do not use light sessions if you or a close family member have epilepsy or have ever had a seizure, or if you are sensitive to flashing or flickering light.
  • Ask a doctor first if you are pregnant, have a heart or mental health condition, take medication that affects the nervous system, or are unsure.
  • Always keep your eyes closed during light sessions and never look directly at the flashlight.
  • Stop immediately and seek help if you feel unwell, dizzy, disoriented, or notice twitching or any unusual sensation.
  • Never use Glow while driving, operating machinery, in water, or anywhere you need to stay alert.
  • Light sessions are not intended for anyone under 18.

Answer the in-app safety screening honestly. It helps reduce risk but cannot rule it out.
