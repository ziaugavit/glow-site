Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version: There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your journal, moods and health data stay on your iPhone. If you choose to share it, Glow sends anonymous usage data and crash reports so we can improve the app. That's off until you say yes, and you can turn it off anytime.

Who we are

Glow is made by Vitalijus Ziauga, an independent developer based in the European Union ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact ziaugav@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Glow stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us:

  • Your first name (if you choose to give it) and your onboarding preferences, used to personalise the app.
  • Your journal entries, moods, check-ins and session history.
  • The outcome of the safety screening (passed or not), with its date. Your individual answers are not stored.
  • Your settings, such as music, haptics, voice and your daily reminder time.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or your computer, this data may be included in that backup, under Apple's terms and your control.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Glow:

  • writes each completed session to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes, and
  • reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, sleep and the moods you log in Apple Health (State of Mind), only to show you your own readings and trends inside the app.

This health data is processed on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never stored outside Apple Health. You can change or revoke access any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

Device permissions

  • Flashlight (camera): used only to switch the flashlight on and off. The camera never records or captures images.
  • Motion: used only during a session, on your device, to stop the light if the phone is dropped.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

To check and restore your purchases, Glow uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives from Apple the details of your purchases (plan, dates, trial, renewals, price and currency) together with a random identifier that Glow creates for your install, plus technical data (app version, iOS version, device model, country of your App Store account, IP address). It never receives your name, email or payment details, and is not linked to any other Glow data. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf under its data processing agreement, which may involve transfer to the United States under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR): providing the purchase you made. Purchase records are kept while you are a customer and as long as tax law requires.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports (optional)

During setup, and anytime in Settings → Share anonymous usage data, you can choose to share usage data. Nothing is sent unless you agree. If you do, Glow uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to collect:

  • How the app is used: screens opened, onboarding steps reached, sessions, breathing exercises and red light programs started, paused and finished (with their length), achievements unlocked, live controls changed (music, haptics, voice), articles opened, and settings changed.
  • Purchases: which plan was viewed or bought, trial starts and renewals, as reported by Apple. Never payment details.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic performance data.
  • Technical data: a random app-install identifier, device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate country/region derived from your IP address.

We never send your name, journal text, notes, moods, how you felt after a session, your safety screening answers or result, your goals, or any Apple Health data. We don't use your device's advertising identifier, we don't use this data for advertising, and we don't combine it with other data to identify you. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms, which may involve transfer to the United States under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for 90 days.

Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR): your consent. Withdraw it anytime in Settings; collection stops immediately.

Reviews and support emails

Glow may show Apple's standard prompt asking you to rate the app; ratings go to Apple. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed (at most 2 years later).

What we don't do

  • No advertising SDKs, and no analytics or crash reporting unless you opt in.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold.
  • No account, so no profile linked to your identity exists on our side.

Your rights

Usage data is anonymous and not linked to your identity, so we usually can't find a specific person's records; if you opted in and want them removed, email us and we'll do what we can. Turning off sharing in Settings stops collection immediately. You can delete everything Glow stores on your device with Settings → Delete my data, or by deleting the app. Under the GDPR you may also contact us, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Glow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may log your IP address for security and delivery, under their own privacy policies. We use no cookies or analytics on this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
