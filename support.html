Getting started

How do I use a light session?

Sit or lie somewhere comfortable. Hold your phone with the flashlight about 10–20 cm in front of your closed eyes, or rest it face up on your chest. Keep your eyes closed for the whole session, start the session, and let the light play across your eyelids. Headphones make the music and voice even better.

I don't see much. Am I doing it wrong?

Not at all. Everyone's experience is different, and it often grows over the first few sessions. Try a darker room, bring the phone a little closer to your closed eyes, relax your face, and try the Explore sessions. Some people mostly feel calm rather than see colours, and that's fine too.

Should my eyes be open or closed?

Always closed. Never look directly at the flashlight. Keep your eyes closed until the light has completely stopped.

My phone gets warm during long sessions.

The flashlight produces some heat. Glow automatically eases off if your phone gets too warm. Remove a thick case and avoid direct sunlight or a hot room for longer sessions.

How do I pause or stop?

Press and hold anywhere on the screen to pause. If you drop the phone, the session stops by itself. The volume buttons simply change the volume.

Safety

Who should not use light sessions?

Do not use light sessions if you or a close family member have epilepsy or a history of seizures, if you are sensitive to flashing or flickering light, or if a doctor has advised you to avoid it. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a mental health condition, or are unsure, check with your doctor first. Stop straight away if you feel unwell, dizzy, or uncomfortable in any way.

What does the safety screening do?

Before your first light session, Glow asks ten questions. If any answer suggests a risk, light sessions stay off and you can still enjoy audio-only sessions and red light. Glow asks again every 90 days. Your answers never leave your phone; Glow only remembers whether screening was passed.

Is red light the same as "red light therapy"?

No. Glow's red light is a calm, blue-free screen light for winding down in the evening. It is not a therapy device and does not make health claims. It changes slowly and never flashes.

Subscriptions and purchases

What does Glow cost?

Glow Premium unlocks everything. Choose yearly (with a 7-day free trial), monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Purchases are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Glow and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Glow, open the plans screen and tap Restore. Glow doesn't need an account.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signing in with your Apple ID.

Your data

What data does Glow collect?

Your journal, check-ins, moods and settings stay on your iPhone. There's no account and no tracking. If you opt in, Glow shares anonymous usage data and crash reports to help us improve it; you can turn that off anytime in Settings. If you allow Apple Health, sessions are saved as Mindful Minutes and your trends are shown from your own Health data, all on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In Glow, open Settings and tap Delete my data, or simply delete the app. Mindful Minutes saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.
