Safety comes first

Glow's light sessions use flickering light. Do not use them if you or a close family member have epilepsy or have ever had a seizure, or if you are sensitive to flashing light. Before your first session, Glow asks ten simple safety questions and keeps light sessions off if any answer suggests a risk. Glow is for relaxation and is not a medical device.
